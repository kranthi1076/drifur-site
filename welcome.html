Quick care guide

Three tools, three tips.

01

Reusable lint roller

  • Use short back-and-forth strokes. Don't press hard.
  • Open the chamber and empty the fur after each use.
  • Works best on couches, beds, car seats and coats.
02

Self-cleaning brush

  • Brush in the direction the coat grows, with gentle pressure.
  • Press the button to push the fur off the pins.
  • A few minutes, a few times a week, means less fur on your furniture.
03

Steel comb

  • Use it after brushing to catch small tangles.
  • Great around ears, paws and the tail.
  • Rinse and dry fully before storing.

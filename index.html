What's in the DRIFUR kit?

Three tools: a self-cleaning deshedding brush for your pet, a stainless steel grooming comb for thicker and longer coats, and a reusable lint roller for fabrics.

Does it remove pet hair from couches and clothes?

Yes. The reusable lint roller collects loose dog and cat hair from couches, bedding, rugs, car seats and other fabric surfaces, and traps it in a chamber you empty in one go.

Do I need refills or batteries?

No. All three tools are reusable. There are no sticky sheets, batteries or charging.

Is it for cats, dogs, or both?

Both. The brush and comb are made for grooming cats and dogs, and the roller lifts cat and dog hair alike. Brush gently in the direction of the coat.

Does it remove cat hair from a couch?

Yes. Roll back and forth over the cushion and fine cat hair collects in the roller's chamber, which you empty into the bin. It also works on bedding, throws, clothes and car seats.

Can I use it on a long-haired cat?

Yes. Use the deshedding brush for loose undercoat, then the wide teeth of the steel comb to work through longer fur and the fine teeth to finish. Go gently around tangles.

Is it a good gift for a pet owner?

Yes. The kit comes in a black-and-gold box that is ready to give, with no wrapping needed.
